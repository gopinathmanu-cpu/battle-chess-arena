# Neon Dominion illustrated film assets

Beta 50 keeps `city.png` and selects `white-cast-rook-v1.png` and
`black-cast-bright-rook-v1.png` for resting characters, plus the updated ready
and attack sheets documented in [Neon live poses](NEON_LIVE_POSE_ARTWORK.md).
The original files below remain preserved. See [current cast artwork](BRIGHT_ANIMATED_CAST_ARTWORK.md)
and [submitted edit prompts](BRIGHT_ANIMATED_CAST_PROMPTS.json).

Created on 2026-10-03 with the built-in imagegen tool. The complete submitted
prompt set, transparency settings, input paths and selected output filenames
are preserved in [neon-cinematic-prompts.json](neon-cinematic-prompts.json).
No CLI/API fallback or external stock pack was used.

## Selected artwork

- `assets/cinematics/neon/city.png`: illustrated city environment.
- `assets/cinematics/neon/white-cast.png`: cyan Aurora battle poses, 2172 × 724,
  real alpha transparency.
- `assets/cinematics/neon/black-cast.png`: magenta Void battle poses,
  2167 × 726, real alpha transparency.

The cast preserves the project's Neon army identities: king, queen, elephant
bishop, mounted knight, chariot rook, and sentinel pawn. The circular chess
bases are removed for the film. Input character sheets are the existing
project-directed illustrations in `assets/characters/fantasy-v4-sources/`.
The two intermediate pose sheets used for the layout edits are retained in
`assets/cinematics/neon-source/` for the source handoff; they are not declared
as runtime Flutter assets.

Outputs were copied intact from Codex's generated-images directory. Layout
correction improved sprite separation, but the returned columns were not exactly
equal. The renderer therefore uses measured normalized source rectangles,
preserving each character's actual aspect ratio. Cropping happens while drawing;
the PNGs were not resized, repainted or processed by a bitmap-editing script.

The generated sheets and city were visually inspected for character identity,
alpha edges, unwanted bases, text, logos and watermarks. Source rectangles keep
complete opaque silhouettes; adjacent bishop/knight soft light at the natural
gutter is trimmed. Rendering checks in both phone orientations are recorded
in the Beta 39 handoff.

Both source cast sheets face right. Beginning with Beta 42, the renderer mirrors
Black's cropped figures horizontally so their faces, mounts and weapons point
left in the fixed right-hand lane, including threat portraits and the finale.
The original PNG files are preserved; this is a drawing transform. Magic and
laser strikes, check warnings and victory effects are drawn procedurally from
verified move and result data, without additional bitmap assets.

## Music and narration

`assets/audio/neon_film_score.wav` is an original 40-second atmospheric
synth score generated by [generate_neon_film_score.py](../tool/generate_neon_film_score.py).
The script uses standard-library oscillators and noise, with no recordings,
imported melodies, copyrighted samples or MIDI. The mono 22,050 Hz PCM loop
is 1,764,044 bytes. Its music level is lowered while narration speaks.

The spoken story is assembled from the immutable recorded game by
`NeonCinematicStory`. Android's installed English voice speaks each scene;
the app supplies no external voice recording. Scene completion waits for the
actual speech completion callback. Captions remain available without a voice.
Pause, seek, route changes and disposal cancel pending narration.

Project licensing follows [the open source policy](../OPEN_SOURCE_POLICY.md);
dependencies and platform speech engines retain their own licences.
