# Kingdom chess sets

The app includes 31 built-in themed sets and the unchanged four Classic styles.
Beta 16 adds the common-height piece and per-set board-contrast pass.

On Home choose **Themed**, then filter **All / Historical / Legendary / Fantasy**.
Swipe the full-board previews or use the arrows. The selected set is remembered
and used for a new game. In an ongoing game, **Game settings → Armies & board**
can choose White's army, Black's army and the board world independently.
Home previews hide role-letter badges so the artwork stays visible at small
sizes; gameplay keeps its existing role labels.

are separate from the existing general Epic Guardians set.

## Readability and interpretation

- Every role uses the same large visual height and shared foot baseline so all
  characters remain readable on a phone. Pawns are deliberately much leaner in
  width than every major role instead of being shorter.
- Black armies retain black or near-black primary costumes, while a shared
  contrast treatment lifts their existing gold, silver, red, blue and other
  accessory details for brighter board-size readability.
- A shared midtone lift keeps faces and small facial details bright at phone
  board size without bleaching pale costumes or turning Black costumes grey.
- Every built-in set has its own muted complementary light/dark board palette.
  The colours avoid near-black squares, keep strong square separation and are
  selected against that set's costume colours. Choosing a board independently
  in **Armies & board** intentionally keeps the chosen board palette.
- **Game settings → Choose board colours** provides separate light- and
  dark-square pickers, vivid presets and a live preview. The board/paintbrush
  toolbar icon toggles that saved palette without changing pieces or scenery.
- Each isolated sprite is centred on a shared foot baseline. Black units face
  the opposing army. Captures, promotions and previews use the same artwork.
- Elephants and chariots are consistent **fantasy chess-role motifs**, even for
  cultures that did not use them historically. These are court/attire-inspired
  interpretations, not exact uniforms or portraits of named rulers.
- Epic and mythological courts are labelled Legendary. Troy represents its
  epic interpretation; it does not assert that the Iliad's events are verified.
- Classic pieces retain standard symbols. Custom Studio sets retain your chosen
  parts and sizes. Artwork choices do not change rules, difficulty or online seats.
- Every world has its own compact, procedurally synthesized soundtrack. A
  selected optional music mood remains selected; no downloaded recordings,
  samples, MIDI or commercial music are included.

## Artwork and licensing safeguards

New illustrations were generated with the built-in image tool from original
descriptive prompts, not copied external images. Selected atlases, complete
prompts and source-crop records live in `assets/characters/kingdoms/`; see
[the asset record](../assets/characters/kingdoms/kingdoms.asset.md).
Local cutout processing was explicitly approved by the user.

See [artwork provenance](ARTWORK_PROVENANCE.md) for the review checklist and
unresolved older reference-origin questions. AI generation and GPL notices do
not guarantee non-infringement or exclusive copyright ownership.
