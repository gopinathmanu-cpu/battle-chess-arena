a precise 6-column by 2-row grid. Top row: white king, white queen, white bishop,
white knight, white rook, white pawn. Bottom row: black king, black queen, black
bishop, black knight, black rook, black pawn. Use crisp polished 2D game icons
based on traditional tournament Staunton silhouettes, with realistic carved
ivory and ebony materials. Keep scale, viewing angle, baseline, lighting,
padding, and cell sizes consistent. Use a genuinely transparent background.
Include exactly one fully visible piece per cell. No board, squares, dividers,
text, labels, letters, numbers, logos, watermark, or extra objects.
